Ephata Choir Privacy Policy



Effective Date: January 02, 2026

The Ephata Choir app is a mobile companion to our website ephatachoir.org, offering easy access to hymns, sheet music, events, recordings.


We do not collect any personal data from users of this app.

  • No login, account, or email is required.
  • No personal information (name, email, location, device ID, etc.) is collected, stored, or shared.
  • The app loads public content from our website securely using HTTPS (handled entirely by Apple's/Android's systems).
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used to collect data.

If you have questions, please contact us through ephatachoir.org.

This policy may be updated from time to time.
